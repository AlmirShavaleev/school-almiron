Topics remain closed and homework remains unpublished. Opening them is a separate teacher action.

`topics.autocheck_solutions_after_finish` opts these topics into withholding answer keys, solution text, solution images and the homework-solution PDF until every task is closed (correct answer or three attempts). Copies inherit the setting from their source topic. Staff retain access; other lessons keep their existing per-task behavior.

The two accompanying migrations are already applied to School; filenames match the remote migration history. Uploads were limited by a fixed manifest and SHA-256 hashes; the temporary upload endpoint was retired after all 184 assets were verified.

Validation: 56 PDFs / 303 pages reviewed, no footer overlap; 89 tasks per course; student-role rollback checks confirmed no early text/PDF access and reveal after finish, without retained answers or grades. Focused frontend tests and the in-memory PostgreSQL gate test cover the new behavior.
